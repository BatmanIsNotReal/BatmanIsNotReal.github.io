BLOOD & EARTH

Feed on the living. Raise a house of vampires. Claim the land, dig into the dark — and hold it against those who come to take it.

Play now See the world

Free. Runs in your browser. No download, no sign-up.

One house. Three ways to bleed.

I

Build your hold

Start with a hut and a thirst. Put up huts, farms, mines and lumber mills on a map you can zoom, scroll and pop out into its own window. Your familiars move in, go to work and wander the paths between your buildings.

Raise vampires to drink for you, spend your attribute points, and let the blood keep flowing while you are away.

II

Descend underground

Find the cave on your land and step inside. Six dungeons, from the Dripping Tunnels to the Black Throne, each with monsters that hit harder than the last. Claw them, burn them, and take their wood, iron and influence.

You share every dungeon with other players in real time. The deepest one, the Abyss, has no rules but your own. Rare helmets drop from the dead — wear them on your character.

III

Hold the line

Once you are strong enough, others take notice. Bandits, nests and clans plant an outpost on your map and begin to raid. Their army marches in slowly, and you can see it coming.

Build watch towers, guard outposts and forts. Send your vampires to destroy the outpost, or lose buildings to the enemy until you take them back.

A closer look

What's in the crypt

Idle at heart

Everything keeps producing while the tab is open, and a slow, ten-second rhythm means no frantic clicking.

A living map

Forest, water, rock and grass. Zoom with the wheel and watch familiars walk between buildings.

Multiplayer caves

Real-time dungeons, shared with other players. Fight side by side, or against each other in the Abyss.

Raids & war

Three factions, early warnings, garrisons and forts. Win a battle and the enemy loses units too.

Blood temple

Offer some of your blood for a chance at a surge of influence.

Your character

Design a face, wear the helmets you find and share a page for your house.

Leaderboard & chat

See who rules the night, talk to other players and gamble a little at the dice table.

Yours to keep

Your game saves in your browser. Nothing to install, no account to make.

How to begin

  1. Name yourself. Pick a vampire name and a house name.
  2. Drink. Press the blood button. Spend it on vampires and familiars.
  3. Build. Raise huts for room, farms for food, mines and mills for materials.
  4. Grow. Gain levels, enter the cave and prepare for the first raiders.
