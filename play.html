      How to play

      Build a vampire house that grows stronger over time.

      1. Drink blood. Click the big blood drop in the corner. Spend blood on vampire animals and vampires: they give you blood and influence every 10 seconds, even while you are away.
      2. Get familiars. Adopting one costs influence. Give each familiar a job with the + and − buttons: human food or wood. They eat 1 food per 10 seconds, so keep some on food. Familiars without a job are the ones who can work in new buildings.
      3. Build on the map. Click a tile to open the build menu. Huts make room for more familiars, farms give food, lumber mills (on forest) give wood, and mines (on rock) give iron, bronze and gold. Click one of your buildings to upgrade it (up to level 10) for more output, more room or stronger blessings. Upgrades cost resources, blood and free familiars. Scroll the mouse wheel over the map to zoom.
      4. Level up. Influence fills your level bar, and resets to 0 each time you level up. Level 2 takes 1,000 influence and every level after that needs more. Iron tools (hoe, axe, pick) make your buildings more productive, and each one you buy makes the next one a little dearer.
      5. Aim high. The Blood Temple is the late-game goal: each round it may bless you with extra influence, but a blessing eats 10% of your blood. Check the leaderboard, chat with other players, and expect a random event now and then.

      The game saves by itself every 10 seconds, so you can close it and come back later.
